Daylight, Sunlight & Overshadowing

Light is a defining factor in building performance, occupant wellbeing, and planning success. Our daylight and sunlight consultancy offers essential guidance for developers, architects, and planning authorities seeking to optimize natural illumination while meeting BRE guidelines.

Using advanced 3D solar simulation software, we analyze microclimate impacts, calculate Vertical Sky Components (VSC), and evaluate Annual Probable Sunlight Hours (APSH). This detailed analysis ensures proposed developments preserve neighbor light rights while maximizing solar gains inside new structures.

Beyond planning compliance, our overshadowing and light-access assessments help refine interior layouts, reduce artificial lighting power demands, and elevate overall architectural comfort.

Landscaping

Integrating outdoor living spaces, drainage planning, and native planting schemes, our landscape solutions transform exterior grounds into harmonious natural extensions of the built environment.
