Daylight, Sunlight & Overshadowing

Our environmental consultancy team delivers authoritative daylight and sunlight assessments required by local planning authorities. Utilizing computer simulations based on official BRE (Building Research Establishment) guidelines, we ensure your proposed development optimizes internal illumination while respecting surrounding properties.

Navigating rights to light disputes and planning restrictions early prevents costly redesigns late in the construction cycle. We produce clear, visual technical reports detailing Daylight Factor, Average Daylight Factor (ADF), Annual Probable Sunlight Hours (APSH), and transient solar shadow paths across surrounding gardens and amenities.

Whether working on single-residence extensions or multi-story urban infill developments, our daylight calculations empower design teams to maximize glass placement, passive solar gain, and compliance with national sustainability standards.
